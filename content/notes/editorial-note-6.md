# Editorial note: connective tissue without over-explaining

The book should be connected by **recurrence and consequence**, not by repeated explanation.

Avoid bridges that say:

> We have now seen X. Next we will explore Y. Together these ideas show Z.

They make the structure obvious, but they also make the reader feel managed.

Prefer connections where:

* one chapter leaves a question open;
* the next begins inside that question;
* an earlier image, phrase or rule returns with changed meaning;
* the consequence of one idea creates the need for the next.

A small gap is useful when it lets the reader make the connection. The goal isn’t to remove every jump, only the accidental ones.

## Transition check

1. Read only the final paragraph of the first chapter.
2. Read only the opening paragraph of the next.
3. Ask what question remains active at the break.
4. Check whether the next chapter recognisably responds to it.
5. Add no more than one short bridge if it doesn’t.

> **Connect chapters by leaving the right question open, not by summarising the answer.**
